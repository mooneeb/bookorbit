# BookOrbit Reading Notes

The language for reading and taking notes across BookOrbit and the personal iPad app.

## Language

**Ink note**:
A reading note that preserves the reader's handwriting or drawings as editable marks.
_Avoid_: Scribble note

**Typed note**:
A reading note expressed as editable text, whether entered with a keyboard or through Scribble.

**Passage-linked note**:
A typed or handwritten note associated with a particular passage in a book.

**Page ink**:
Handwriting or drawings placed directly over the book's displayed content.

**Annotated view**:
A preserved view of book content in the layout in which page ink was created, together with its editable ink.

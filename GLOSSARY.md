# BookOrbit Reading and Book Management

The language for reading, taking notes and managing books across BookOrbit and the personal iPad app.

## Language

**Book management**:
Importing, describing, organizing and removing books in the reader's library, individually or in bulk. It is distinct from server, account, permission and device administration.

**Book merge**:
An explicitly confirmed combination of two existing library entries into one book, retaining their reading notes and personal histories.

**Surviving book**:
The library entry chosen to represent the combined book after a book merge.

**Merge review**:
The reader's review of competing personal content retained when book entries are merged.

**Personal book note**:
The reader's single general note about a book, separate from text-linked notes, page ink and journal entries.

**Journal entry**:
One entry in the reader's whole-book journal. A book can have several journal entries, each separate from annotations on selected words or PDF pages.

**Reading attempt**:
One occasion of reading a book, with its own dates and outcome.

**Reading history**:
The reader's retained record of reading a book, including separate attempts, sessions and their original library context.

**Image-only page**:
A book page whose words are stored as an image rather than as selectable book text.

**Reflowable EPUB**:
An EPUB whose text changes its line and page layout to fit the reader's chosen text size and available space.

**Fixed-layout EPUB**:
An EPUB whose pages keep the publisher's designed positions for text and images when viewed at different sizes.

**Partial reading**:
Access to the readable parts of a book with its unavailable content or features explicitly identified.

**Offline book**:
A book the reader has chosen to keep available for reading without a server connection.

**Reading-note recovery backup**:
A restorable copy of the reader's notes, bookmarks, editable handwriting, saved context and local changes, including work not yet synced to BookOrbit.

**Ink note**:
A reading note that preserves the reader's handwriting or drawings as editable marks.
_Avoid_: Scribble note

**Typed note**:
A reading note expressed as editable text, whether entered with a keyboard or through Scribble.

**Text-linked note**:
A reading note attached to an exact selection of words, whose text, editable handwriting, or both stay separate from the book's changing layout. Reselecting the same range reopens its note; different ranges have separate notes, even if they overlap.
_Avoid_: Passage-linked note, page note

**Highlight**:
A visual marking of selected words, optionally accompanied by a text-linked note. Its appearance can be removed while keeping the note and its selected range.

**Page ink**:
The editable handwriting and drawings placed directly on one PDF page, grouped into one entry in the reading notes.

**Embedded PDF annotation**:
A highlight, drawing or comment stored inside a source PDF, separate from the reader's BookOrbit notes and page ink.

**Saved context**:
A book excerpt or annotated page kept with a reading note so the note remains understandable after the book is replaced or removed.

**Source version**:
The particular EPUB or PDF content used by a reading note, bookmark or reading position. Replacing that content creates another source version; changing text size or reading layout does not.

**Note conflict**:
Competing content brought into one reading note by edits, a book merge, or an edit arriving after Trash. The competing versions remain attached to one note for review rather than becoming separate reading notes.

**Conflicting version**:
A version of a reading note preserved for comparison and resolution alongside another version. Preserving it does not restore a note from Trash.

**Recovered changes**:
Local edits kept after their reading note was permanently deleted or their bookmark was removed. They stay separate from active notes and bookmarks unless the reader explicitly saves them as a new item of the same kind.

**Bookmark**:
A deliberately saved text location or book page with its own label, separate from the automatically saved reading position and from a text-linked note. A fixed-layout EPUB page can be bookmarked even when it has no selectable words.

**Book contents**:
The chapter or section navigation supplied by a book, including a PDF's document outline. It is separate from the reader's personal bookmarks.

**Reading preferences**:
The reader's device-specific choices for book appearance and reading mode, with defaults and optional choices for individual books.

**Reading position**:
The location where the reader stopped in a particular source file, used to resume reading. It is distinct from a note's selected words or PDF page-ink placement.

**Competing reading positions**:
Different places reached independently on devices, kept as alternatives until the reader chooses where to continue.

**Text-anchored ink**:
The historical EPUB experiment in which handwriting drawn over words was intended to follow them across changing layouts. It is distinct from current PDF page ink and handwriting contained in a text-linked note.

**Writing mode**:
A reader state in which Pencil input creates or edits ink.

**Annotated view**:
A preserved view of book content in the layout in which page ink was created, together with its editable ink and original context.

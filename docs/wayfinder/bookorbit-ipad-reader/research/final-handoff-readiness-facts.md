The older readiness report correctly records its earlier controls/profile gaps but is historical after their confirmed resolutions. Final indexes should use current resolution comments while preserving dated research and rejected probes. [Stale reader status][old-status], [earlier audit][earlier-audit].

No glossary implementation leakage or core scope contradiction was found. One terminology risk was the historical Text-anchored ink definition using Page ink, which the current glossary makes PDF-specific. The final assembly clarifies the historical term rather than reopening the rejected general EPUB experiment. Source version also applies consistently to the already-confirmed notes, bookmarks and reading positions. [Pinned vocabulary][glossary], [accepted hybrid][hybrid].

## Evidence qualifications retained

- Original-context preservation covers named scratch fixtures and qualified human outcomes. The first offline state was human-reported and browser-version provenance was incomplete. It is not the production store/download/sync system. [Preservation evidence][preservation].
- Browser proof used local delivery, in-memory typed saves, fixture-specific PDF mapping and fixed 2x derivatives. The public default EPUB association differs from the private native-authored exact-range fixture; some derivatives were rendered with macOS PencilKit. Editable erase/Undo success does not imply byte-identical archives after edits. [Browser evidence qualifications][browser-evidence].
- Signing proof covers prototype manual IPA renewal/update with retained identity/data. Ordinary badge/background renewal, reverse Xcode transition and the production reader's network/credential/pending-state continuity were not exercised. [Signing limits][signing].
- No performance, application restore, migration, production integration or new physical reading/accessibility result is supplied by this audit. Required available-text accessibility remains mandatory. Completed fixture gates need no reopening merely to publish the final specification.

Final assembly and the user's complete shared-understanding confirmation remain prerequisites to resolving the journey decision and completing the planning map. The audit authorizes no production work.
